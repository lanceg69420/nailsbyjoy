Gel X Extension

Gel-X, also known as soft gel extensions, are lightweight full-cover nail extensions comprised of soft gel. They provide a natural appearance, suppleness, and durability without the use of acrylics or hard gels. When used with a specific gel adhesive, they provide a smooth finish and can last up to 3–4 weeks.

Builder Gel (BIAB)

Builder gel (BIAB — Builder in a Bottle) is a thick, durable gel designed to strengthen and protect natural nails. It promotes nail growth and can be utilized for overlays or short extensions, providing a strong yet flexible finish.
